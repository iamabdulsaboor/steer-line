FAQS

Frequently asked questions

How much does the modification cost?

Several factors contribute to the price, including the type of modification and also the type of steering wheel. The work is priced individually.

Can I buy a steering wheel?

Yes, we offer various steering wheels for purchase. Please contact us for available models and pricing.

Do I have to send in my steering wheel and immobilise the car?

Typically, you would send your steering wheel to us. We aim for quick turnarounds to minimize the time your car is immobilised. We can also discuss temporary solutions.

How long does the modifications take?

Modification times vary depending on the complexity. We provide an estimated timeframe upon consultation.

Will you modify my steering wheel?

Yes, we specialize in modifying a wide range of steering wheels. Contact us with your specific model for details.

Will the steering wheel from, for example, a Golf 7 fit in a Golf 8?

Compatibility depends on specific car models and years. We can advise on whether a particular swap is feasible and safe.

What exactly do you do?

We perform custom modifications, re-upholstering, carbon fiber applications, and electronic upgrades to steering wheels.

Will the display work in my car?

We ensure all displays and electronic functions are fully compatible and integrated into your vehicle.

What sets you apart from your competition?

Our commitment to top-quality materials, individual order handling, and expert craftsmanship ensures superior results.

Do you send to my country?

Yes, we offer international shipping. Please contact us for shipping costs and delivery times to your specific country.

Can you make me a steering wheel cheaper?

We focus on quality and durability. While we offer competitive pricing, we do not compromise on the materials or craftsmanship.

Where can I fit a steering wheel?

We recommend professional installation to ensure safety and proper functionality. We can provide guidance or recommend installers.

Will I lose my car warranty after modification?

It's important to check your car manufacturer's warranty terms. Our modifications are designed to integrate seamlessly, but warranty coverage can vary.

Where can I place an order?

You can place an order directly through our website, via email, or by phone.

How can I pay?

We accept various payment methods, including bank transfers, and major credit cards. Specific options will be provided during the order process.

The steering wheel has a vibration or heating function. Are you able to maintain such functions?

Yes, we can usually maintain all existing functions, including vibration and heating, during our modifications.

Is it possible to make larger paddle shifters?

Yes, custom larger paddle shifters are a popular modification we offer. We can discuss various designs and materials.

How long have you been in the market?

We have been passionately serving the automotive customization market for several years, building a strong reputation for quality.

What are the 3 options?

Please contact us to discuss the "3 options" you are referring to, as this might be specific to a current promotion or package.

When do I have to pay?

Payment terms are typically a deposit upfront with the balance due upon completion and before shipping. Full details will be provided in your quote.
