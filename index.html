How do we operate

The new steering wheel - three possibilities

We tailor specifically to your needs. You choose how you want your steering wheel to look.

First option

  • Send us your original Steering Wheel® - modified to your exact specifications.
  • Photos sent for approval before payment.
  • After payment, your modified Steering Wheel will be sent to you.

Second option

  • Pay a holding deposit – new OEM Wheel modified to your specs.
  • Photos sent for approval before payment.
  • After installation, send your original wheel to release your deposit.

Third option

  • Keep your original Steering Wheel – pay upfront for a new OEM Wheel.
  • Photos sent for approval before payment.
  • Once approved, your new Steering Wheel is shipped.
