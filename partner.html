Partnership program

Welcome to our partnership program, designed to make the process of fitting our custom steering wheels faster, easier and more economical for our customers.


We know how difficult it can be to find reliable and reputable garages to fit our steering wheels to our customers’ cars, so this partnership program is designed to help solve that issue.


Our partners provide excellent customer service and competitive prices. We take customer satisfaction seriously and consider it our top priority. We value the input of our customers and look to our partners to ensure that they get the best service possible.


If you’re a garage that would like to become one of our partners, please don’t hesitate to contact us. We would love to have you as part of our network.


We take customer satisfaction very seriously and are always looking for more ways to make the fitting process easier for our customers. We value your feedback and insight, so please don’t hesitate to reach out if you have any questions or suggestions about the program.


Thank you for your interest in our partnership program. We look forward to working with you and helping our customers find the perfect steering wheel for their cars.
